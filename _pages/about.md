# Yukun Chen · 陈煜坤

I work at the intersection of photonics, electronics and computation. My recent research at Zhangjiang Laboratory focused on a 16-channel microring-based computational spectrometer and high-speed optical interconnects. I studied Physics at the University of Oxford and have been admitted to the MSE in Electrical Engineering at the University of Pennsylvania.

My interests include silicon photonics, optical sensing and spectroscopy, optical interconnects, and optoelectronic systems that combine devices, circuits and algorithms. [Email me](mailto:chenykfrank@outlook.com) · [GitHub](https://github.com/chenykfrank)

## Research experience

### Zhangjiang Laboratory · Research Intern
*Computational spectroscopy and optical communications · Shanghai · July 2025 – July 2026*

- Developed an O-band computational spectrometer using a 16-channel electrically tunable microring array. The estimated spectral resolution is approximately **0.1 nm**; this is an estimate, not a measured system accuracy.
- Integrated a tunable laser, photodetectors, multichannel power supplies and microring devices. Calibrated the channel responses and wrote Python tools for wavelength and bias sweeps, data acquisition, response-matrix calibration and spectrum reconstruction.
- Implemented and tested an FPGA-based optoelectronic interface in Xilinx Vivado, and designed a KiCad PCB connecting the photonic chip, transimpedance amplifiers and FPGA.
- Simulated an **800 Gbit/s DWDM** optical link in VPIphotonics and assisted with silicon-photonic device characterization using a VNA, source-measure units, lasers and optical power meters.

### University of Oxford · Research Intern
*Atomic and Laser Physics Laboratory · Diffractive optical computing · June – September 2024*

- Built a physics-informed diffractive optical neural network in PyTorch with free-space propagation and trainable optical modulation layers.
- Investigated layer-wise training and compared gradient-based optimization with wavefront matching in accuracy, computation and optical efficiency.
- Aligned free-space optical components and supported experimental calibration.

### Shanghai Artificial Intelligence Laboratory · Research Intern
*Large language models · July – September 2023*

- Curated physics datasets for domain-specific language-model training.
- Fine-tuned Llama 2 with LoRA and applied Direct Preference Optimization; improved performance on the physics portion of MMLU by approximately **20%** in the internship evaluation.
